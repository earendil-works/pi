# Escalation

Stop before acting when scope is unclear or a decision changes architecture, security, privacy, provider selection, deployment, persistence, external integration, or authority boundaries.

Create a record using `templates/escalation.md` in `docs/escalations/`. State the situation, exact decision required, options, trade-offs, recommendation, and blocked work. Escalate to Joshua W. Dorsey, Sr. Do not proceed until required approval is explicit.
