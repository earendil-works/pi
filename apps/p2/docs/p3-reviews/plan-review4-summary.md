# Review round 4 (Fable, Astra): the full plan as of `c991bdf02`, summary for the owner

Both reviewed every decision of 2026-10-21 as one design. Verdict: keep one public `WatchHandle` and one small
synchronous publication mechanism; drop the `replicatedState` bridge; defer IndexedDB. Nothing below is decided.
