# Intent Logging

Before a broad-scope, sensitive, externally visible, architectural, or difficult-to-reverse action, create a record from `templates/intent-log.md` under `docs/agent-log/intent/`.

Describe exact action, rationale, affected trust boundaries, risks, alternatives, and whether human approval is required. If approval is required, stop and escalate. Complete the outcome section after the action.
